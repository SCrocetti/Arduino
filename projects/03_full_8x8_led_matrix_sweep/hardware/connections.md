## 🇺🇸 English
### Leave Unconnected
The following pins on the 1088BS matrix are not used in this 6x6 sweep:
* **Rows:** Pin 9 (R1), Pin 5 (R8)
* **Columns:** Pin 13 (C1), Pin 16 (C8)

### Arduino Wiring

#### Rows
| Row | Matrix Pin | Arduino Pin |
|:---:|:----------:|:-----------:|
|  2  |     14     |      2      |
|  3  |      8     |      4      |
|  4  |     12     |      6      |
|  5  |      1     |      8      |
|  6  |      7     |     10      |
|  7  |      2     |     12      |

#### Columns
| Column | Matrix Pin | Arduino Pin |
|:------:|:----------:|:-----------:|
|    2   |      3     |      3      |
|    3   |      4     |      5      |
|    4   |     10     |      7      |
|    5   |      6     |      9      |
|    6   |     11     |     11      |
|    7   |     15     |     13      |
